# Privacy Policy

*Draft for review by Ride Coop's lawyer before publishing. Last updated: {{date}}.*

Ride Coop is operated by Caritech Solutions Inc., Barbados ("we"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Ride Coop apps, website, store portal and fleet portal, and what we do with it. We handle personal data in line with the Data Protection Act of Barbados.

## What we collect
- **Account:** your name, mobile number, email address, and photo if you add one.
- **Location:** while you book and during a trip, the location of your phone, so drivers can find you and the route and fare can be worked out. Drivers share their location while they are online.
- **Trips and orders:** pickup and drop-off places, times, route, fare, tips, ratings, messages and calls between you and your driver made in the app (we don't record call audio).
- **Payment:** cards are saved with our payment provider; we keep only the card type, last four digits and expiry. Drivers, fleets and stores give bank details for payouts.
- **Drivers:** licence, permits, vehicle documents and photos you upload for approval.
- **Device:** a notification token and basic technical information to keep the apps working.

## How we use it
- To provide rides, deliveries, payments and payouts; to keep riders and drivers safe (PIN check, SOS, trip sharing); to give support; to prevent fraud; and to meet legal and tax duties.
- We send you messages about your trips and account. We only send marketing if you agree, and you can stop it at any time.

## Who we share it with
- **The other person on your trip:** riders see their driver's first name, photo, car and plate and the car's location; drivers see the rider's first name, pickup and drop-off, and the rider's location while coming to pick them up. Phone numbers are kept private when masked calls are on.
- **Service providers** who work for us: hosting, maps (Google), text messages (Twilio), email, push notifications (Firebase) and the card payment provider — only what they need to do their job.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## How long we keep it
- Account details are kept while you have an account. If you delete your account, your name, number, email, saved places, saved cards, and drivers' documents and bank details are removed. Trip and payment records are kept without your details for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

## Your choices and rights
- You can see and change your details in the app, ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or delete your account in the app (Menu → Delete account) or at {{delete_url}}.
